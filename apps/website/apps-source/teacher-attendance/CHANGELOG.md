# Teacher Attendance

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Daily register for teaching and administrative staff, marked present, late,
  absent or excused, with minutes recorded for a late arrival.
- Marking the same person twice on one day corrects the entry rather than
  adding a second one.
- Monthly summary per member of staff for the administrator.
- Works offline: the register loads from cache and marks are queued until the
  connection returns.

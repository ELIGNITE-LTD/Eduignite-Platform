# Front Desk

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Sign visitors in at reception: name, phone, purpose and who they are visiting.
- See who is currently in the building; sign them out on the way past.
- Sign-in and sign-out times are stamped by the server, so the record cannot
  be back-dated from the browser.
- Daily visitor log for administrators, any date.
- Works offline: the desk loads from cache and sign-ins are queued until the
  connection returns.

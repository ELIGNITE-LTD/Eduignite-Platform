# EduIgnite System Description

## 1. Introduction

**EduIgnite is a complete school management and learning platform** that brings every part of running a school — students, teachers, parents, classes, marks, fees, communication, and more — into one simple, connected system.

EduIgnite was developed to replace the scattered mix of paper registers, spreadsheets, printed report cards, WhatsApp groups, and manual cash records that most schools rely on today. These tools don't talk to each other, information gets lost, and staff spend hours on work that could be automated.

EduIgnite solves the everyday problems schools face:

- **Disorganised records** — student, staff, and academic information kept in books and files that are hard to search or keep up to date.
- **Slow, unclear communication** — important messages to parents and students getting lost or delivered late.
- **Time-consuming admin work** — manually calculating marks, writing report cards, tracking attendance, and chasing fee payments.
- **Lack of transparency** — parents rarely knowing how their child is really doing until the end of term.
- **Difficult fee collection** — cash handling, lost receipts, and no clear record of who has paid.

By putting all of this in one secure place — accessible on the **web, on Windows computers, and on Android phones**, and even **usable offline** — EduIgnite helps schools work faster, communicate better, and give parents and students the clarity they deserve.

---

## 2. Vision

EduIgnite's long-term vision is to become the **trusted digital backbone of every school**, making quality school management and modern learning tools available to institutions of every size.

The platform aims to transform school management and learning by:

- **Turning administration into automation** — so school leaders spend less time on paperwork and more time on education.
- **Connecting the whole school community** — administrators, teachers, students, and parents working from the same, always-current information.
- **Bringing transparency to education** — where a parent can see their child's progress, attendance, and results the moment they are recorded.
- **Making advanced tools accessible** — including artificial-intelligence assistants that were once available only to large, well-funded institutions.
- **Supporting schools as they grow** — from a single school to networks of schools managed under one roof.

The goal is a future where running a school is simpler, learning is better supported, and every stakeholder is informed and engaged.

---

## 3. Objectives

The main objectives of EduIgnite are to:

1. **Centralise school information** — keep all student, staff, academic, and financial records in one organised, searchable place.
2. **Automate routine tasks** — attendance, mark calculation, report cards, ID cards, fee receipts, and reminders.
3. **Improve communication** — provide instant, reliable messaging and notifications between the school, teachers, students, and parents.
4. **Increase transparency** — give parents and students real-time visibility of results, attendance, assignments, and fees.
5. **Simplify fee management** — support mobile-money payments, automatic receipts, and clear payment records.
6. **Support teaching and learning** — with assignments, online exams, a digital library, and AI-powered study and guidance tools.
7. **Work everywhere, reliably** — across web, desktop, and mobile, with offline support so work continues even without internet.
8. **Enable multi-school management** — allow founders and administrators to oversee one school or many from a single platform.

## 4. Who Uses EduIgnite?

EduIgnite is built around the real roles found in a school. Each user sees a workspace tailored to what they need.

### School Administrator
The person who runs the school day to day. They manage students, staff, classes, subjects, the timetable, announcements, fees, and school settings. EduIgnite gives them a single command centre to oversee everything and make decisions with up-to-date information.

### Teacher
Teachers manage their classes and subjects. They record attendance, enter marks, create and grade assignments, set online exams, and communicate with students and parents. EduIgnite removes much of the manual work so teachers can focus on teaching.

### Student
Students access their classes, assignments, exam schedules, marks, report cards, timetable, and the digital library. They receive notifications, submit work, take online exams, and can use AI study tools — all from their phone or computer. Every student can also be issued an official, QR-verified student ID card.

### Parent
Parents get a clear window into their child's education. They receive **every notification sent to their child** — assignments, results, announcements, and fee information — and can follow attendance, progress, and payments. This keeps parents informed and involved without needing to visit the school.

### Accountant / Bursar
The finance officer manages school fees and payments. They record and track who has paid, accept **mobile-money payments**, issue professional receipts, send payment reminders, and view revenue summaries — replacing cash books and loose receipts with clear, reliable records.

### Librarian
The librarian manages the school's book collection and lending. They track which books are borrowed, by whom, and when they are due, and the system automatically flags overdue loans and notifies the borrowers.

### Super Administrator / Founder (Executive)
For those who own or oversee one or more schools (for example, a founder or head office). They manage the whole platform: registering and configuring schools, overseeing school networks, controlling licensing, viewing strategic insights across schools, and — where authorised — moving a school to a recoverable "draft" state or permanently removing it. Executive users see platform-wide information rather than the internal announcements of any single school.

## 5. Core Features

### Student Management
Create and maintain complete student records — name, class, date and place of birth, guardian details, admission number, and photo. Each student gets a unique registration number and can be issued an **official bilingual (French/English) student ID card** with a scannable QR code that anyone can use to verify the student's identity. *Used by administrators and teachers; benefits students and parents.*

### Teacher & Staff Management
Register and manage teachers and other staff, assign them to classes and subjects, record staff remarks, and organise reporting structures. This gives the school a clear, accurate picture of its workforce. *Used by administrators.*

### Parent Management
Link parents and guardians to their children so that parents automatically receive information about their child and can follow their progress. *Used by administrators; benefits parents and students.*

### Class & Subject Management
Set up the school's classes, levels, and subjects, and assign teachers to each. This forms the structure that attendance, marks, timetables, and report cards all build upon. *Used by administrators.*

### Attendance
Record and track student attendance and view attendance summaries over time. Reliable attendance data supports discipline, safety, and reporting. *Used by teachers and administrators; visible to parents.*

### Timetable
Build and manage class timetables and lesson periods, giving teachers and students a clear schedule of when and where classes take place. *Used by administrators; viewed by teachers and students.*

### Marks Management & Report Cards
Enter marks for assessments, and let the system calculate averages and produce **professional report cards** ready to print or share. This eliminates manual calculation errors and hours of paperwork at the end of each term. *Used by teachers and administrators; benefits students and parents.*

### Assignments
Teachers create assignments with instructions, attachments, and deadlines; students submit their work; teachers grade it and give feedback. Teachers can even use an **AI helper to draft an assignment**. Students and their parents are notified automatically when new assignments are set. Older assignment files are automatically cleared after a period, keeping records tidy while preserving the scores. *Used by teachers and students.*

### Exams
Run **online exams**, including AI-assisted exam generation and a reusable question bank. Students take exams online, results are recorded, and marks can be pushed directly into the school's records. *Used by teachers and students.*

### Communication (Messaging & Group Chat)
A modern, familiar messaging experience for the school community. Users can hold direct conversations and **group chats**, with group administrators who can add members, choose whether everyone or only admins may post, and appoint other admins. It shows when someone is typing, who is genuinely online, and supports **emoji reactions** on messages, as well as sharing photos and documents. *Used by everyone.*

### Notifications
Timely alerts for assignments, results, announcements, fees, and more. Notifications can be targeted to specific roles, and — importantly — **every notification sent to a student is also sent to their parents**, so families never miss important information. *Used by everyone.*

### Announcements
Schools can publish announcements to the whole school or to specific groups. Parents and students see the announcements relevant to them, keeping the community informed. *Published by administrators and teachers.*

### Fees & Payments
Manage school fees, record payments, and accept **mobile-money payments**. The system issues branded receipts, sends payment reminders, and produces revenue summaries — making fee collection transparent and far easier to manage. *Used by the accountant/bursar and administrators; benefits parents.*

### Library
A digital catalogue of the school's books with lending and return tracking. The system automatically identifies overdue books and reminds borrowers. *Used by the librarian; benefits students.*

### Community
A community directory that showcases the school's staff and students. Teacher profiles can display the **classes and subjects each teacher teaches**, helping everyone understand who does what within the school. *Used by the whole school community.*

### AI Features
EduIgnite includes a suite of artificial-intelligence tools that support both administration and learning, such as:
- An **AI assistant** for questions and guidance.
- **Study plans** tailored to a student's subjects.
- **Performance and attendance insights**.
- **Exam preparation** and **AI-assisted exam and assignment creation**.
- **Career orientation, guidance reports, skill-gap analysis, and academic roadmaps** to help guide students' futures.
*Used by teachers, students, and administrators.*

### Settings & School Configuration
Schools configure their own details, academic year, class levels, branding, and an **academic calendar**. Every user can edit their own profile. This lets each school tailor the platform to how it actually works. *Used by administrators and all users.*

### Additional Modules
EduIgnite also includes **live classes**, a **testimonies and ratings** area, **training resources (how-to videos)**, a **support** channel, **feedback** collection, **academic rewards and promotions**, **strategic insights and overview dashboards**, and **draft/recovery management** for schools — rounding out a complete school ecosystem.

## 6. How the System Works

EduIgnite is used through a simple, secure login. Each person signs in and immediately sees a workspace built for their role. It can be used in a web browser, as a Windows desktop application, or as an Android app — and it keeps working **offline**, syncing automatically once the connection returns.

**A School Administrator's day:** signs in to a dashboard overview, adds or updates students and staff, sets up classes and the timetable, posts an announcement to parents, checks fee collection, and reviews school-wide insights — all in one place.

**A Teacher's day:** signs in, takes attendance for the day's classes, enters marks, creates or grades an assignment, sets or reviews an online exam, and replies to messages from students or parents.

**A Student's day:** signs in, checks new assignments and the day's timetable, submits completed work, views marks and report cards, borrows from the library, and uses AI study tools to prepare for exams.

**A Parent's day:** signs in (or simply receives notifications), sees their child's latest results, attendance, and announcements, follows assignment deadlines, and checks or makes fee payments.

**An Accountant's day:** signs in, records and confirms payments, accepts mobile-money transactions, issues receipts, sends reminders to families with outstanding fees, and reviews revenue reports.

**A Librarian's day:** signs in, lends and receives books, and lets the system handle overdue tracking and reminders automatically.

Throughout, information entered by one person is instantly available to everyone who should see it — a mark entered by a teacher appears for the student and parent; a payment recorded by the accountant updates the family's record.

## 8. Real-Life Use Cases

- **End of term without the stress.** Instead of spending nights calculating averages and handwriting report cards, teachers enter marks and the system produces professional report cards ready to share.
- **A parent stays informed.** When a teacher sets an assignment or publishes results, the parent receives the same notification as the child — no more surprises at the end of term.
- **Paying fees from home.** A parent pays school fees by mobile money and instantly receives a proper receipt, while the accountant's records update automatically.
- **Verifying a student's identity.** A staff member or partner scans the QR code on a student's ID card and instantly confirms the student is genuinely enrolled.
- **Running an exam online.** A teacher generates an exam with AI support, students take it online, and the results flow straight into the school's marks — no printing or manual entry.
- **Keeping the library in order.** The librarian lends a book with a tap, and the system automatically flags it if it becomes overdue and reminds the borrower.
- **A quick school-wide message.** An administrator posts an announcement, and the right students and parents are notified immediately.
- **Working through a power or internet cut.** A teacher continues recording attendance and marks offline; everything syncs automatically when the connection returns.
